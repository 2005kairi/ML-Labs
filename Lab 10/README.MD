The analysis of the Iris flower dataset involved exploring the data using a pairplot and KDE visualizations to observe how the three species: Setosa, Versicolor, and Virginica—separate across feature space. After splitting the dataset into training and testing sets, an SVM classifier was trained and evaluated, achieving about 98% accuracy based on the confusion matrix and classification report. To further improve performance, GridSearchCV was applied to tune key hyperparameters such as C and gamma. Overall, the results showed that Setosa is the most easily separable species, displaying clear boundaries compared to the other two classes.
